# NDT Brokerage — Lead Generation & Agency Platform

## What It Is

A two-sided platform / brokerage business that connects NDT service buyers (large organizations, construction firms, utilities) with NDT service providers (NDT companies and solo NDT contractors). Péter acts as an intermediary, earning a commission on matched deals.

The name "Burstview" is mentioned as a possible product/entity name in raw1.

## The Opportunity

Buyers of NDT services want fast, reliable quotes from vetted providers. NDT providers want clients without having to do their own sales. Péter has:
- Deep relationships on both sides of the market
- The ability to deliver quotes in 30 minutes (vs industry standard of days)
- Knowledge of the full Hungarian NDT provider landscape

Key stat: **40% of NDT contracts are won by speed of quoting alone** (Balogh Dávid). This means whoever delivers the first credible quote has a structural advantage regardless of price.

## The Model

### Supply Side (NDT Providers)
- Round 1: NDT service companies (non-NDT companies that provide NDT services, without own capability — "bocsátásmentes")
- Round 2: individual NDT contractors (egyéni vállalkozó anyagvizsgálók)
- They list capacity, pricing, availability
- Confidentiality maintained: buyers don't see their rates directly

### Demand Side (Buyers)
- Large organizations: utilities, construction maintainers, manufacturer quality labs
- They send project requests; Péter's system aggregates quotes from multiple providers
- They receive consolidated quote options within 30 minutes

### Revenue Model
- Commission on matched deals
- Potentially subscription or listing fee for providers

## Competitive Advantage

1. **Speed**: 30-minute quote delivery is unprecedented in Hungarian NDT market
2. **Relationships**: Péter personally knows the key players on both sides
3. **Market intelligence**: knows which buyers are risk-averse, which are price-sensitive
4. **AI processing**: competitor prices collected, processed overnight, quotes out next morning

## Capacity Tracking Feature (Later Stage)

Once providers are in the system, Péter envisions real-time capacity visibility:
- See which providers are booked vs available
- Proactively offer them work when they have gaps
- Buyers benefit from knowing actual available capacity before requesting quotes
